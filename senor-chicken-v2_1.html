Our Story

Authentic Flavor,
Family Heart

Señor Chicken is Aurora's beloved Mexican rotisserie grill — a family-run restaurant dedicated to fresh ingredients, authentic recipes, and generous portions that keep you coming back.

From our fire-roasted rotisserie chicken to our signature street tacos and hand-crafted burritos, every dish is made with the same care our family puts into everything we do.
